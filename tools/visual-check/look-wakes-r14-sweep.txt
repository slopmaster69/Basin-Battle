# Wakes, eighth round (client 0085): a sweep of the open water's knobs (`lookwater tune`), so
# the surface's numbers are picked from the client itself rather than from the CPU model (which
# missed the fine texture twice). The open water of look-wakes-r14-lake-above's first shot: the
# hero still at the spit's tip, WoW (-9461, -333), facing west; the camera yaw 90 (looking west),
# pitched 76 down, 18 yd back; noon, clear, sun shadows at the grade.
# It holds still on the defaults for 3.5 s, then steps one knob at a time through a few values,
# 3 s each, the rest at their defaults, and ends on the defaults again. Each value has its own
# still, named for it (`milk-0.2-00.jpg`: milk at 0.2), and client.log logs every change
# ("[look water] tune set: milk set to 0.2 (default 0.35)") and, at the start and the end, every
# knob ("Knobs: ..."), so the video's frames can be matched to the values by time.
# The knobs swept (defaults in brackets; `lookwater tune <name>` says what each holds):
#   milk [0.35]: the shallows' milky veil, the most of the bed's light it takes;
#   milk_tone [0.85]: the shallows' milk colour's brightness (the seventh filming's 155);
#   relief [0.2]: how much the 0.36 and 0.2 yd wavelets shade the water (the embossed look; 0.5
#     was the seventh round's);
#   mid [0.5]: the 0.36 yd wavelets' weight (1 was the seventh round's);
#   grain [0.25]: the new fine grain, about 0.1 yd (7 px on these films);
#   grain_yd [0.1]: its wavelength;
#   milk_depth [1.2]: the depth (yd) by which the veil is fully in (0 from the waterline);
#   sky [0.85]: the mirror.
# PLEASE send client.log's "[look water]" lines with the video.
# Look for, at each value: the shallows' brightness (about 120 to 130 wanted) and how plainly the
# bed shows under the first yard; the fine grain (an even fine texture, no embossed dents); the
# deep's brightness and movement (right on the seventh filming: keep them).
