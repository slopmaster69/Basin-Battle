# Tripwire (2): laid between the hunter and the dummy, then Reverse Grapple hauls the dummy across.
# Since #110 the hunter places each arrow: the first press plants one ahead and to the left, a
# ring shows how far the wire reaches, and the second press plants the other ahead and to the right.
# Look for: the first arrow and its ring, the second arrow inside it, the rope running between the
# two arrows (not across them), and the dummy tripping on it (#110: 2).
hero hunter
include _setup
5.0 .es slot 2 tripwire
5.2 .es slot 1 grapple_arrow
5.5 .es valor full
6.0 .es in ab 3 0                    # the first arrow
6.4 .es in a {facing+0.6} -0.5       # the ground about 3 yd ahead, to the left
6.5 .es in au {facing+0.6} -0.5
7.0 .es in ab 3 0                    # the second, inside the ring
7.4 .es in a {facing-0.6} -0.5       # and to the right: the wire across the dummy's way
7.5 .es in au {facing-0.6} -0.5
6.2 shots laid 20 0.1
8.5 .es in ab 2 1                    # Reverse Grapple: the dummy dragged over the wire
9.0 .es in a
9.1 .es in au
8.8 shots tripped 35 0.06
