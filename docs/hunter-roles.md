# Hunter: every spell has a job

The Hunter, rebuilt the way the other classes were: every spell is a card with its own deck and
an Epic, every spell has one job, rarity means something, and a Specialization (Beast Mastery,
Marksmanship or Survival) turns up as a Legendary card with its own spells and path cards. The
full list is in [hunter-cards.md](hunter-cards.md) (generated from the game's files).

A marksman with a wolf at their side: the wolf fights your target (or the nearest enemy) and
comes back after a short rest if it falls. No mana: every spell is a cooldown.

## The spells and their jobs

| Spell | Its job | What you feel without it |
|---|---|---|
| Arcane Shot | The shot on the run: instant, and magic, so no armor stops it. Harder on the poisoned. | Nothing to fire while kiting. |
| Serpent Sting | The poison: venom on everything; without a target it seeks an enemy not yet stung. | Arcane Shot stays small. |
| Claw | Your wolf's strike: it leaps on your target and keeps it busy. | The wolf just bites. |
| Multi-Shot | The spread: arrows into the pack. | Packs take longer. |
| Concussive Shot | The slow: keep them off you and off the Ancient. | Runners reach you. |
| Hunter's Mark | **The team's call**: the marked takes 8% more from everyone (more from you and your pet), and the Ancient's defences turn on it and hit it 25% harder. | The team spreads its damage. |
| Volley | The rain: arrows on a pack, over and over. | Packs take longer. |
| Explosive Trap | The ambush: bursts into flame when they walk on it. | Nothing waiting at the choke. |
| Freezing Trap | The lockdown: what walks on it stops. | Leaks. |
| Furious Howl | The rally: you, your pet and allies deal more damage. | The team hits softer. |
| Feign Death | The escape: whatever hunts you loses interest. | One bad pull ends you. |
| Scatter Shot | The peel: stuns the target and everything beside it. | Enemies stay on your allies. |

Gone as spells: Raptor Strike (a melee strike for a ranged class) and Mend Pet (now a class
talent: your pet regenerates and comes back sooner).

## Specializations (Legendary, one a run, at random)

| Specialization | What it does | Opens these spells | Path deck |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beast Mastery** | Your pet deals 30% more, has 30% more health, comes back 3 sec sooner. | Bestial Wrath (the pet's rampage), Intimidation (the pet's leap and stun) | 11 cards: Unleashed Fury, Ferocity or Spirit Bond, Kill Command, Frenzy... Epic: **The Beast Within** |
| **Marksmanship** | 5% more crit, crits 25% harder, Trueshot Aura (the team deals 5% more). | Aimed Shot (the sniper's shot), Rapid Fire (the burst) | 11 cards: Lethal Shots, Careful Aim or Barrage, Master Marksman, Quick Shots... Epic: **Marksmanship Mastery** |
| **Survival** | Traps 25% stronger, 10% less damage taken, 10% faster. | Wyvern Sting (the sleeper), Deterrence (the stand) | 11 cards: Clever Traps, Lightning Reflexes or Thick Skin, Lock and Load, Entrapment... Epic: **Apex Predator** |

The Hunter has no interrupt (the Warrior, Mage and Shadow Priest have one); its team job is the
mark, the traps and the howl.

## Some cards that change how the Hunter plays

- **Lit keys**: Lock and Load (trap hits light up Arcane Shot), Thrill of the Hunt (Multi-Shot
  through 4 lights up Arcane Shot), Kill Command (crits light up Claw), Steady Focus (the mark
  lights up Aimed Shot and Arcane Shot), Trap and Shoot, Master Marksman (instant Aimed Shot).
- **Choices**: Arcane Shot splinters, doubles or becomes Chimera Shot; Claw stuns, mauls or
  growls (the target turns on your wolf); Feign Death leaves Disengage, a Snake Trap or a Flare.
- **Epics**: Arcane Barrage, Serpent Swarm, Kill Command, Storm of Arrows, Frost Shot, Mark of
  the Wild Hunt, Arrowstorm, Dragonfire Trap, Glacier, Call of the Wild, Shadowmeld,
  Scatterstorm, Aspect of the Beast, Stampede, Sniper Shot, Bullseye, Wyvern Nest, Aspect of the
  Turtle.

## Icons

Bestial Wrath and Wyvern Sting pointed at icons your WoW client doesn't have; they use ones it
has now (a roaring beast, a drowsy sting).

## Balance

Bots playing the same games (20 seeds): defend 17.1 waves, expedition 19.9 (16.75 and 19.75 before).
Each upgrade adds 6% to its spell (as the Warrior's). Through level 40 the Hunter's own damage is about a quarter
lower than the old Hunter's (more of its spells are utility: slows, traps, the mark, the howl);
by level 60 it matches it against one target and beats it against packs.
