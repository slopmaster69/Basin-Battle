# Cookie: dinner interrupted

Claimed for the agreed Deadmines design. Cookie commits a directional pour that leaves one
cooling patch, then scoops from his pot after a count of completed exchanges. Breaking posture
during the scoop spills the stew and opens a long cough recovery. An uninterrupted scoop gives
a bounded small heal and a committed short steam cone. Waiting remains a viable answer.

Gorged inhales, commits a frontal belch, and coughs through a long recovery. The old health-line
summons and frenzy are removed. Every danger must show its preparation and recovery with floor
markers disabled, for 1 to 4 heroes and any loadout.

Implementation scope: a narrow kitchen module, Cookie/Gorged content blocks, minimal shared AI
and lifecycle hooks, focused simulation tests, and an isolated controlled visual-check scenario.
Native murloc animations, pot props, and steam effects will be checked against local data before
claims about presentation. No client patches or other boss implementations are owned here.

This first commit records the claim only. Implementation, release checks, and client verification
remain outstanding. No footage or normal-health dungeon balance is claimed.
