# Warrior: the feel pass

Every level-up card was graded on one question: **will the player feel it in the next ten
seconds of play?** Cards that failed were made bigger, made visible, or cut.

> A later pass gave every skill one job of its own, Epic capstones, path-only skills and path
> cards: see [warrior-roles.md](warrior-roles.md). Some cards named below have since moved to
> another skill, been renamed or been cut; [warrior-cards.md](warrior-cards.md) is the current list.

## What makes a card feel good

1. **You can see it.** Something new happens on screen: an extra target, a shockwave, a stun,
   a bleed ticking, a buff icon, a key that lights up, the card's name popping over the bar.
2. **You notice the rhythm change.** A cooldown that goes from 8 to 6 to 4 seconds, a cost that
   goes to nothing, a skill that comes back on a crit or a kill.
3. **The number is big enough to see.** Rank-ups are 20-50% a rank (taken twice), not 12% taken
   three times. Cost cards cut 5-6 rage at a time, or make the skill free.
4. **Some quiet cards earn their place.** "Enemies deal less damage" (Demoralizing Clap,
   Weakening Wound) can't be seen as a number, but the simulated teams held about two fewer
   waves without them, so they stayed, stronger (25% and 20%), as the tank's cards.
5. **It works right away.** Bonuses that only count at a bridge post, near the Ancient or
   against elites stay only where that situation is common (bosses, defending), and are big.
6. **No invisible stats.** "+3% critical strike chance a stack", "+5% haste a stack", "1% heal
   per hit" and "6 yd more reach on a melee strike" were cut or folded into something bigger.

## New things the game can now do (for every class)

- **Lit keys (empowered casts).** A proc can light up a skill's key: its cooldown finishes at
  once, the key glows and pulses, and the next cast is free, stronger, or usable on any target
  (Execute at any health, Revenge without waiting for a blow). Pressing it uses it up.
  Examples: Riposte, Sudden Death, Eye of the Storm, Bloodsurge, Sword and Board, Vengeful Fury,
  and combinations like Heroic Leap (Charge lights up Heroic Strike) and Storm and Thunder
  (Thunder Clap lights up Whirlwind).
- **Callouts.** When a card's effect goes off in a big way (a reset, a free cast, a blast, a
  lit key, a big heal or shield, a team buff), its name and icon pop over the spell bar and drift
  up, so you know which card just did that. Small steady effects (a little rage per hit) stay
  quiet.

**Class talents:** three-rank talents of 2-4% each became one card that matters (Cruelty 8%
crit, Toughness 10%, Anticipation 15% health, Weapon Mastery 8% a rank, Commanding Presence 15%
faster cooldowns). Bleeding Frenzy and Unrelenting Assault (a few rage) were cut; Battle Flow
now resets the skill that crit. Four new talents need one skill and light it up: **Sudden
Death** (Execute), **Victory Rush** (each kill lights up Heroic Strike, free and double damage),
**Revenge Ready** (blocks light up Revenge) and **Bloodsurge** (big Whirlwinds and Thunder Claps
light up Bloodthirst).

**Combinations:** the ones that were only a quiet percentage now make something happen: Mortal
Wounds (Rend lights up Mortal Strike), Blood Frenzy (big Whirlwinds light up Bloodthirst),
Thunderous Rend (Thunder Clap makes everything bleed), Crippling Wounds (Hamstring lands a free
Rend), Opening Strike (Heroic Strike crits light up Execute), Storm and Thunder, Exposed Throat,
Sweeping Death, Double Strike, Concussive Force, Sundering Slam, Armor Breaker, Defiant Fury and
Punishing Revenge. Two are new: Deathbringer and Shield and Thunder.
