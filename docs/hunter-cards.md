# Hunter: every level-up card

_Generated from `content/classes/hunter.toml` by `tools/card_catalog.py`. Edit the content file, not this page._

- **18 skills**, up to 6 on your bar at once.
- **270 skill upgrades** (about 15 per skill). A skill takes **10** in a run, so no run sees a whole deck.
- Every upgrade also makes its skill hit 6% harder, on top of what the card says.
- **33 class talents** (offered whatever skills you hold; some come back for more ranks).
- **30 combinations** (offered only while you hold both skills).
- **3 specializations** (one a run, at random, as a Legendary card). It teaches nothing by itself: it opens its own skills to the skill cards (on keys of their own, so a full bar never shuts them out) and its own **33 path cards**.
- **Rarity means something**: Common cards are numbers, Uncommon change how you play, Rare light a key, reset or cast, and each skill's **Epic** capstone comes only after 5 of its upgrades (a path's after 4 of its cards). Skills themselves are not rolled for rarity.

How to read the upgrade tables: **Rank-up xN** can be taken N times. **Choice: x** means only one upgrade with that choice can be taken for the skill. **After: Y** is offered once Y is taken. **Lights up a key** makes a skill's next cast ready at once and empowered (its key glows until you press it). What each skill is for: [hunter-roles.md](hunter-roles.md).
