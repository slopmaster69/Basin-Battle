# Priest, draft 3

Corey's review of draft 2 (Nov 6 2026), Claude's and ChatGPT's answers to it, and Corey's calls
on them. Most of draft 2 is gone; what's left each asks for a different decision.

**Corey's rulings, kept:** Power Word: Shield is the heart of the class (the only way a Priest
saves a friend from a blow). Priests can't parry. The weapon decides the clicks. Priests aren't
teleporters. No Mage looks on any Priest spell. Damage over time is the Warlock's. No interrupts.
Vampiric effects heal the Priest only. A spell's state shows on the spell, not in a gauge.
**New:** every spell is worth taking alone (no spell that only works with a group), and no spell
moves an ally without asking.

**Gone:** Holy Fire, Leap of Faith, Mind Grip, Void Shift, Dispersion, Grace, the 2 sec Heal,
Flash Heal's bolt, Drain, Pain on the shadow bolt. **Benched** (rules kept, off the board): Mind
Flay and Mind Sear.

## The staffs

A player spends most of a fight on the clicks, so each staff is a playstyle with a loop.

### Acolyte's Staff (holy): damage feeds healing

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Click | **Smite**: a bolt of holy light, 9 damage. Marks the foe with **Searing Light** for 5 sec. |
| Hold click | **Greater Smite**: charge up to 1 sec, 9 to 22 damage; let go on the flare for a perfect (26, and it pierces). Held past the flare it weakens back to 12. Let go in the air, it throws you back 4 yd (once a jump). |
| Hold right click | Aim a heal (the reticle turns gold). **Click while aiming: Healing Gust**, a slow wave of light that heals every ally it passes through for 20. If it meets no ally, it heals you for 15 as it fades. 2 charges, 10 sec each. |
| Searing Light | Any other Priest spell or a Greater Smite striking a marked foe tears the mark out: 8 more damage, and Healing Gust's recharge jumps 3 sec. |

Healing never waits on damage (Gust recharges by itself); good damage makes it come back faster.

### Shadowcaller's Staff (shadow): focus and patience

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Click | **Shadow Bolt**: 9 damage. Each hit weaves a stack of **Shadow Weaving** into the foe (6 sec, up to 5); each stack makes your Shadow Bolts 8% stronger on it. |
| Patience | 2 sec without firing, the staff darkens: the next bolt is **Empowered**: half again as fast, 10 yd farther, 1.5 times the damage, and 4 Valor on a hit. |
| Hold click | **Shadow Burst**: a charged bolt, 14 to 26, its flare a perfect (as before). |
| Right click | **Dark Reckoning**: rips the Weaving out of the first foe along the reticle, 7 damage a stack (35 at 5). Cooldown 8 sec. |

### Staff of the Hollow Star (void): tend a living orb

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hold click | **Void Tether**: a cord to the first body along the reticle, 20 yd. On a foe: 6 damage a sec, rising to 12 over 2 sec. On a friend: 4 healing a sec, rising to 8. On your orb: 6 health a sec, and the orb drifts toward you while held. Breaks past 23 yd or out of sight. |
| Right click | **Hollow Star**: throws a dark orb to the reticle, 15 yd. It has 40 health (up to 60) and lasts 10 sec. Every second it deals 5 to each foe and heals each friend 5 within its reach (3 yd, up to 5 as it fills). Damage it deals fills it; healing it gives drains it. Foes can break it. Standing in it, you run 15% faster. Cooldown 18 sec. |

## Light (choice 1)

| Key | Spell | EX (Valor) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q | **Penance**: the timing | **Greater Penance** (25): 5 rings, quicker |
| E | **Holy Nova**: standing together | **Surge of Light** (25): bigger, and friends run faster |
| 1 | **Angelic Feather**: the road out | **Body and Soul** (25): everyone near you, at once |
| 2 | **Power Word: Shield**: the save | **Power Word: Barrier** (25): the dome |
| 3 | **Holy Word: Salvation**: the group's beat | |

- **Penance** (cooldown 9 sec). Up to 3 bodies in front of you (30 yd, in sight) are picked: friends
  who are hurt and foes, taking turns. A ring closes on each in turn (0.55 sec apart). Press Q as a
  ring closes and a bolt of light flies to that body: 14 damage to a foe, 14 healing to a friend;
  dead on the beat, 18. A beat missed is that bolt lost; the rest still come. All three on time is
  the perfect (Valor and a burst of light). You keep moving and rolling the whole time: the skill
  is the timing while you fight, not aiming the camera.
- **Holy Nova** (cooldown 9 sec). Light bursts 8 yd around you: 16 damage to foes, 16 healing to you
  and friends; each other hero inside makes it 25% stronger (up to 75%). No timing check: where you
  stand is the skill.
- **Angelic Feather** (2 charges, 20 sec each: one every 10 sec over a fight). A feather of light at
  the reticle (20 yd), or at your feet. The first hero to touch it (you or a friend, by choice) runs
  40% faster for 3 sec and slows and roots on them end. It lasts 15 sec; 2 can lie at once. Lay
  your own escape, or a road for a friend.
- **Power Word: Shield** (2 charges, 24 sec each). Absorbs 30 for 6 sec on the friend under the
  reticle, or you. Shield a friend just as a blow lands on them and it's parried for them: the
  Priest's perfect, with its own holy burst (no Mage looks).
- **Holy Word: Salvation** (100 Valor, 40 sec). A 10 yd circle where you stand; you rise on wings of
  light, can't be harmed or move, but can still fight from the air. Three times a ring runs **out**
  from you to the rim (2, 1.5, 1 sec); every hero inside who acts as it reaches the rim lands the
  beat: a stack of **Holy Light** (10% more damage a stack, up to 3, 10 sec). The third calls a
  pillar: 30 damage, plus 15 for each hero who landed it (110 at most), and heals each of them 20.
  Alone, you land the beats yourself.

## Shadow (choice 2)

| Key | Spell | EX (Valor) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q | **Mind Blast**: prediction | **Shadow Word: Death** (25): judgement |
| E | **Psychic Scream**: space | **Psychic Horror** (25): more of it |
| 1 | **Fade**: the sidestep | **Greater Fade** (25): it weakens them |
| 2 | **Shadowguard**: take it, give it back | **Vampiric Touch** (25) |
| 3 | **Void Rift**: gather, then implode | |

- **Mind Blast** (cooldown 8 sec). A dark sigil on the ground at the reticle (30 yd), its edge and
  its heart drawn still; 0.9 sec later it erupts. Four hits (Corey's review, Nov 6): the edge
  (2.5 yd) 24; the heart (1 yd) 30; the heart on a foe on the move, the read that was right, 36
  and 5 Valor (the perfect); a foe with Shadow Weaving or a Warlock's shadow on it takes 25% more
  of any of them (45 at most). Lead your target.
- **Shadow Word: Death** (EX, 25). A lance at the first foe along the reticle: 18, or 45 to a foe
  below 20% health. If it doesn't kill, 12 comes back at you. A kill gives one free Death within
  3 sec (that one gives no other).
- **Psychic Scream** (cooldown 18 sec). Every foe within 6 yd flees for 3 sec (heroes 1.5); damage
  ends it. **Psychic Horror** (EX): 9 yd, 3 sec (heroes 1.5). The Warlock's fear is one foe, aimed; this one is
  everyone around you, and it breaks.
- **Fade** (cooldown 10 sec). A quick sidestep 5 yd the way you're moving (back if you're not), in
  Fade's shadow; nothing touches you for 0.4 sec. A blow that misses because of it: the striker is
  slowed 40% for 2 sec (shadow clings to its legs) and you run 30% faster for 1.5 sec. **Greater
  Fade** (EX): 7 yd, and the striker deals 20% less for 3 sec.
- **Shadowguard** (cooldown 12 sec). A shell of shadow for up to 2 sec: you take 50% less, move at
  half speed, and can't attack. What it stops is stored (40 at most). Press 2 again (or wait it out)
  and the stored shadow flies at the first foe along the reticle. Let go within 0.3 sec of a blow
  landing on you: 1.5 times as much (45 at most), the perfect.
- **Vampiric Touch** (EX, 25). As before: a hand of shadow on the foe in front of you; 20% of what
  you deal it heals you, 8 sec.
- **Void Rift** (100 Valor, 40 sec). A wound in the ground at the reticle (30 yd), 4 yd wide and
  growing to 7 over 2 sec; cords of shadow drag foes 3 yd a sec to its heart (heroes 5 yd at most),
  6 damage a sec. Press 3 again to implode it: 40, plus 8 for each foe caught (80 at most). With 3
  or more caught, the perfect: 20 Valor back. Left alone, it collapses after 4 sec for 20.

## Looks (direction)

Every Priest perfect gets a holy burst of its own; nothing borrows the Mage's Arcane Explosion.
State shows on the body or the ground: Penance's closing rings on the bodies, the feather on the
ground, the Weaving's shards on the foe, the darkened staff, the Shadowguard's shard over the
shoulder, the rift's cords. New images (rings, feather, rift, shard, Weaving) come from Corey's
ChatGPT chat as before.
