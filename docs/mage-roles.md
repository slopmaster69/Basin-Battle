# Mage: every spell has a job

The Mage, rebuilt the way the Warrior was: every spell is a card with its own deck, every spell
has one job, rarity means something, and a Specialization (Fire, Frost or Arcane) turns up as a
Legendary card with its own spells and path cards. The full list is in
[mage-cards.md](mage-cards.md) (generated from the game's files).

The Mage has no mana: every spell is a cooldown, and many have a cast time (stand still). Magic
ignores the armor elites and bosses wear, so the Mage is the one who melts them without a
Warrior's Sunder Armor.

## The spells and their jobs

| Spell | Its job | What you feel without it |
|---|---|---|
| Frostbolt | The chill: its target 30% slower; frost hits harder on the chilled and the Frozen. | Runners reach you. |
| Fireball | The big hit: the hardest of the basic spells, slow to cast, leaves a burn. | Single targets take longer. |
| Arcane Missiles | The elite melter: a channelled volley that can't miss, 30% stronger against elites and bosses. | Elites and bosses take longer. |
| Arcane Explosion | The panic button: instant, all around you, the one blast you can cast on the run. | Whatever reaches you stays on you. |
| Frost Nova | The freeze: everything within 10 yd stops for 4 sec (and the Ancient's defences hit the Frozen 30% harder). Your escape. | Melee packs catch the glass cannon. |
| Fire Blast | The instant shatter: fire's only instant, twice as hard on the Frozen. Frost Nova, then Fire Blast. | No burst on the frozen. |
| Scorch | Fire Vulnerability: every fire spell on the target hits harder, yours and anyone's. | Fire never heats up. |
| Blizzard | Holds a lane: a long storm, everything in it 40% slower. | Packs walk through the bridge. |
| Flamestrike | The clump killer: a pillar of flame, then burning ground. | Big packs take longer. |
| Cone of Cold | The peel: everything in front of you 50% slower for 6 sec. | Enemies stay on your allies. |
| Blink | The way out. | Stuck. |
| **Counterspell** *(new)* | The interrupt: breaks an enemy's cast up to 30 yd away. | Soul Bolts land, Dark Menders heal their pack, boss rituals finish. |

Counterspell works like the Warrior's Shield Slam but needs no path: when a breakable cast is in
reach, the enemy's cast bar pulses **INTERRUPT!** and the Counterspell key lights up. Its deck
builds on breaking casts (Backlash, Arcane Shatter, Quick Wit, the Spellsteal Epic).

## Specializations (Legendary, one a run, at random)

| Specialization | What it does | Opens these spells | Path deck |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Fire spells crit 10% more often; crits Ignite (30% of the crit over 4 sec). | Pyroblast (the sniper's shot), Combustion (the burst window) | 10 cards: Fire Mastery, Pyromania or Burning Soul, Hot Streak, Living Bomb, Blast Wave... Epic: **Firelord** |
| **Frost** | 30% more crit against the Frozen, frost spells 20% more against the chilled, 10% less damage taken. | Ice Barrier (the shield), Cold Snap (every frost spell ready again) | 10 cards: Shatter Mastery, Ice Armor or Deep Winter, Fingers of Frost, Frozen Core... Epic: **Winter's Heart** |
| **Arcane** | Cooldowns 15% faster, 10% Clearcasting, arcane spells 10% stronger. | Arcane Power (the burst), Presence of Mind (the instant) | 10 cards: Arcane Echoes, Instability or Fortitude, Missile Barrage, Slow... Epic: **Archmage** |

The specialization spells ride on keys of their own when your bar is full, as the Warrior's do.

## Some cards that change how the Mage plays

- **Lit keys that make a cast instant** (new for every class): Brain Freeze (Frostbolt), Heating
  Up (Fireball), Shatter Setup (Frost Nova lights up Fire Blast and Frostbolt), Hot Streak
  (Pyroblast), Mind of Fire / Frost / Arcana (Presence of Mind), Time Warp (every spell).
- **Choices** in most decks: Frostbolt splinters, fires twice or becomes the instant Ice Lance;
  Fireball explodes, doubles or becomes the Great Fireball; Blink leaves arcane, frost or fire
  behind you.
- **Epics**: Glacial Spike, Meteor, Arcane Torrent, Supernova, Absolute Zero, Flame Lance,
  Inferno, Eternal Winter, Rain of Fire, Frozen Wastes, Shimmer, Spellsteal, Living Sun, Phoenix
  Flames, Ice Block, Deep Freeze, Arcane Ascendance, Time Warp.
- **For the team**: Arcane Intellect, Dampen Magic and Amplify Magic auras; Rallying Silence,
  Kindle the Pyre, Arcane Empowerment, Glacial Embrace, Winter's Embrace; Scorch's Fire
  Vulnerability helps every fire spell; Frost Nova and Arcane Nova expose what they hit.

## New things the game can now do (for every class)

- A card's trigger can **freeze**, **set burning**, and leave **Fire Vulnerability**, **Winter's
  Chill** or **Shadow Vulnerability** stacks; it can watch for **burning**, **afflicted**
  (shadow) and **poisoned** enemies.
- A lit key can make its spell's next cast **instant**.
- A kill's blast goes off where the enemy fell (it used to go off around you).
- Each class names its paths: Weapon Path for the Warrior, Specialization for the Mage.

## Balance

Bots playing the same games (20 seeds) with the new Mage reach about the same depth as before:
defend 16.4 waves (16.55 with the old Mage), expedition 19.65 (20.95). Each upgrade adds 3% to its
spell (the Warrior's add 6%): the Mage's cards are big on their own. Late in a run (level 60) the
Mage hits about 30% harder than the old one, most of it against packs.
