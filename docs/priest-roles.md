# Priest: every spell has a job

The Priest, rebuilt the way the Warrior and the Mage were: every spell is a card with its own
deck and an Epic, every spell has one job, rarity means something, and a Specialization (Holy,
Discipline or Shadow) turns up as a Legendary card with its own spells and path cards. The full
list is in [priest-cards.md](priest-cards.md) (generated from the game's files).

The Priest keeps the team standing: heals and shields land on whoever needs them most, and
even the damage spells can mend. No mana: every spell is a cooldown.

## The spells and their jobs

| Spell | Its job | What you feel without it |
|---|---|---|
| Smite | **Atonement**: holy damage that mends. A quarter of every Smite heals the most hurt ally near you. | Damage and healing compete for your casts. |
| Shadow Word: Pain | The spreader: pain on everything; without a target it seeks an enemy not yet in pain. | Packs take longer. |
| Mind Blast | The burst: the big shadow hit, half again as hard on enemies in pain. | No burst. |
| Holy Nova | The panic button: hurts every enemy and heals every ally around you. | Swarmed with nothing to answer. |
| Power Word: Shield | The barrier: a shield on the ally who needs it most. | Spikes kill your tank. |
| Renew | The slow heal: cheap, steady. | Everyone slowly bleeds out. |
| Flash Heal | The emergency: a big heal, quickly. | Nothing for the moment it goes wrong. |
| Holy Fire | The burn: Smite hits burning enemies harder. | Smite stays small. |
| Mind Flay | The snare: its target dragged to a crawl. | Runners reach the Ancient. |
| Psychic Scream | The scatter: everything near you flees. | Swarmed. |
| Prayer of Healing | The group heal: everyone hurt near you, at once. | Pack damage wears the team down. |
| Devouring Plague | The drain: a long plague that heals you. | You need someone to heal you. |

Heals still mend hired defenders standing beside the healed and bless them (30% more damage for
8 sec).

Damage over time can now mend allies too (Vampiric Embrace, Shared Suffering): every tick heals
the most hurt ally near the priest.

## Specializations (Legendary, one a run, at random)

| Specialization | What it does | Opens these spells | Path deck |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Holy** | Holy spells (heals and Smite alike) 15% stronger, 10% more crits. | Lightwell (a well of healing), Greater Heal (the big heal) | 11 cards: Spiritual Mastery, Divine Fury or Serenity, Spirit of Redemption, Surge of Light... Epic: **Avatar of Light** |
| **Discipline** | Shields absorb 25% more, cooldowns 10% faster, the team takes 5% less. | Power Infusion (power for an ally), Inner Focus (a sure crit, no cooldown) | 11 cards: Shield Mastery, Mental Strength or Power of the Soul, Rapture, Atonement (all your damage mends)... Epic: **Archbishop** |
| **Shadow** | Shadowform: shadow spells 20% stronger, 15% less damage taken, 10% of your shadow damage heals you and allies. | **Silence** (the Priest's interrupt), Vampiric Embrace (shadow that heals the team) | 11 cards: Shadow Weaving Mastery, Shadow Armor or Twisted Faith, Shadowy Insight, Blackout... Epic: **Voidform** |

Silence works like Counterspell and Shield Slam: when a breakable cast is in reach, the enemy's
cast bar pulses **INTERRUPT!** and the Silence key lights up. Shadowform no longer forbids holy
spells: it's the Shadow specialization's passive now.

Gone as spells: Desperate Prayer and Inner Fire (both are class talents now), Shadowform (the
Shadow specialization).

## Some cards that change how the Priest plays

- **Lit keys**: Surge of Light (Smite lights up an instant Flash Heal), Rapture (shields light up
  Flash Heal), Shadow Orbs and Shadowy Insight (Mind Blast), Empowered Fire (Holy Fire lights up
  Smite), Focused Light / Shadow / Wrath (Inner Focus), Spirit Shell (every spell).
- **Choices**: Smite stuns, doubles or becomes Holy Word: Smite; Power Word: Shield spreads,
  reflects or bursts; Psychic Scream stuns in place, terrifies or screams for damage.
- **Epics**: Penance, Shadow Plague, Psychic Horror, Circle of Light, Pain Suppression, Echo of
  Light, Guardian Spirit, Holy Inferno, Mind Flay: Insanity, Mass Hysteria, Sanctuary, Black
  Plague, Font of Light, Miracle, Power Overwhelming, Spirit Shell, Mass Silence, Dark Ascension.
- **For the team**: Power Word: Fortitude, Shadow Protection, Divine Spirit and Prayer of Spirit
  auras; Light's Embrace, Power Word: Barrier, Divine Hymn, Well of Hope / Fury, Rallying Silence.

## Icons

Some of the old Priest spells pointed at icons your WoW client doesn't have (Holy Nova, Power
Infusion, Lightwell showed a question mark). They use icons the client has now.

## Balance

Bots playing the same games (20 seeds): defend 16.75 waves, expedition 19.75 (16.4 and 19.65 with
the old Priest). Late in a run the new Priest hits about a third harder against one target and
takes less damage; against packs it is about the same.
