# Priest art: a request for the desktop chat

From the cloud chat (Cloud POV), which built the Priest (#206, #207, #208). Corey asked the desktop chat to make the Priest's interface art the way it made the Mage's: continue Corey's ChatGPT image chat (https://chatgpt.com/share/6aed0c93-a57c-83e8-abf0-7a268cd556cb) with the prompt below, in the same style as the Mage's rings and meters.

## How to hand them back (the two chats can't message each other)

1. Save each image as a PNG named by its number and name, e.g. `P1-salvation-ring.png`.
2. Commit them on this branch, **`claude/priest-art`**, beside this file in `art-src/priest/` (the PNGs as ChatGPT made them, on black; the cloud chat turns black to alpha and makes the TGAs).
3. Comment on **#206** with the branch name. The cloud chat watches #206 and wires them into the add-on.
4. Or, if it's easier, convert them yourself the way `art/check/` was made and put the TGAs in `addon/ElwynnSurvival/art/check/priest_*.tga` on that branch.

## The prompt (paste as is)

This next collection is for the Priest, keep it in theme with our super badass fantasy Elden Ring style, and matching the Mage's rings and meters you made above (same proportions where it says "match").

Same rules as last time: one asset per image, pure black background, flat and facing you, no words, square.

Isolated on a pure black background, centred, flat front view facing the camera, no perspective, no text, no letters, no border, no drop shadow, game interface asset, crisp shapes with a soft glow, square.

Light is radiant gold and white, warm, sacred, carved stone and sunlight. Shadow is violet and black, smoke and tendrils, never green.

P1. Salvation's ground ring
A ground-based spell indicator matching the fire, frost and arcane ground rings exactly in diameter and thickness: consecrated pale stone cracked with radiant gold-white light, fine geometric sigils (not letters) etched around it, light rising from the cracks, a continuous bright gold-white timing line, the centre empty black.

P2. Salvation's closing rim
A single thin ring of golden light, the same diameter as P1, with a brighter leading edge and a faint trail of motes; nothing inside it.

P3. Salvation's centre sigil
A radiant holy sigil: an eight-pointed star inside a circle, gold-white, glowing, small enough to sit at the centre of P1.

P4. Holy Light stack
A small faceted golden gem with a white-hot core and a soft halo, like a drop of sunlight.

P5. A mote of Grace
A single small orb of warm white-gold light with a soft halo and two faint sparkles.

P6. Grace's halo
A thin radiant halo ring with short rays of light all around it, gold-white, delicate.

P7. A perfect Power Word: Shield
A round shield of light (a bubble seen flat) bursting with white-gold light at the moment it turns a blow, a ring of light flaring out from it, sharp and triumphant.

P8. Mind Flay's tension gauge
An arc-shaped gauge spanning about 120 degrees, the top part of a circle (matching the frost meter's frame in size), made of twisted violet-black shadow with writhing tendrils at both ends, an empty channel running along its middle.

P9. The taut zone
A short segment of that channel glowing pale violet-white, the energy in it stretched thin and trembling, as if about to snap.

P10. The snap
A cord of shadow whipping apart in the middle, its frayed ends sparking violet-white, a few black fragments flying.

P11. A bolt of Penance
A short golden dart of holy light pointing up, a white-hot tip and a soft trail.

P12. Kindled (Holy Fire)
An emblem of holy fire: a gold-white flame with an orange heart rising from a small sunburst.

P13. Fade
A smear of violet-black shadow in the shape of a vanishing silhouette's motion (no face, no person), streaks trailing to one side.
