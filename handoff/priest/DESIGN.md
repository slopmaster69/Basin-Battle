# Priest kit, draft 2: Light and Shadow on the caster core

For Corey, Chain and Serg. The direction is Corey's (the conversation of 5 Nov 2026); the numbers fit the board's budgets and are first guesses to tune in play. Draft 1's Shadow ideas are folded in where Corey kept them.

**The one-line pitch.** The Mage plays against the timing of its own spells. **The Priest plays against the timing of its friends' fights.** Light reads the blows landing on allies and answers them; Shadow pulls a cord taut and keeps it on the edge of snapping.

**Corey's rulings this draft is built on:**

1. **Power Word: Shield is the heart of the class.** Shield an ally as a blow lands on *them* and it's parried for them. The Priest watches how the enemy engages an ally. Discipline in one spell.
2. **Priests can't parry.** No ward, no block: a squishy caster who must dodge. The roll stays. (Rogue may try the same later.)
3. **The weapon decides the clicks, not the class.** A holy staff's right click is a small healing bolt (Ana-style); a shadow staff's is a drain. Neither has a guard: whoever wields one gives up their parry, Priest or not.
4. **Grace** is the Light Priest's thread: perfects build it, the Heal spends it as cast speed and overhealth.
5. **Heals have cast times.** Priests are WoW's casting healers.
6. **The Hymn is holy, not music:** one big circle the whole group plays together, three beats; the reward is each hero's own, and the team part scales with how many land it, so one miss never ruins it for everyone.
7. **Mind Flay is tension:** strongest at the edge of its range, snapping past it.
8. **Fade rewards the dodge:** foes slowed, the Priest quicker.
9. **Vampiric Touch heals the Priest only.** Shadow wants to deal damage.
10. **No interrupts** (the "punish" check was cut: Priests weren't interrupters).
11. **Ki Pulse is saved** for the hybrids (Shaman, Druid, Paladin).
12. **Quadrant bosses** (split up and parry the blows from four sides) are a Priest's weak spot, on purpose; a perfect Shield on the friend covering a side counts as that friend's parry.

## The caster core, Priest-shaped

| Action | Mage (staff) | Priest (holy staff) | Priest (shadow staff) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Left click** | The staff's bolt | **Smite**: a bolt of holy light | **Shadow bolt** that hangs **Pain** on the foe |
| **Hold left** | A charged bolt | **Heal**: a 2 sec cast on the ally under the reticle (yourself if none) | A charged shadow bolt |
| **Right click** | Ward | **Flash Heal**: a small, fast healing bolt; the first ally it meets is healed | **Drain** (hold): a short shadow cord to the foe under the reticle, drawing health to you |
| **Dodge** | Phase step | The roll | The roll |

**The danger:** a Priest has no guard. Casting Heal slows you, a heavy hit breaks it, and the only answer to a blow is not being there: the roll, Fade, or a friend in the way.

## Grace (Light's thread)

- **Earned:** every Priest perfect raises Grace a level, up to III: a perfect Shield, a Penance that flicks between friend and foe, a Holy Nova or Leap of Faith on a blow, each ally landing a Salvation beat.
- **Lost:** a blow that hurts the Priest costs a level. A Priest who lets enemies reach them loses their power (Chain's "in danger up close").
- **Fades:** 10 sec after the last level gained.
- **Spent:** by the next **Heal** (the holy staff's hold):

| Grace | The Heal |
| --- | --- |
| I | Casts 30% faster |
| II | 60% faster, and healing past full health stays on as **overhealth** (a shield, 8 sec) |
| III | Instant, and twice the overhealth |

- **Looks:** motes of light circling the Priest's shoulders, one per level; at III a halo (Inner Fire's glow, 588). The Heal at III lands with a column of light and a chord.

### Q (ranged)

**Light: Penance.** *The flick.*
- Three bolts of light over 1 sec, each fired along the reticle **when it fires**: the first ally it meets is healed 12, the first foe hurt 12. Sweep the reticle between a friend and a foe and the bolts follow.
- **Perfect:** the three land on both a friend and a foe. Grace, and the last bolt bursts in a ring of light.
- Cooldown 8 sec. Range 30 yd.
- *EX* **Holy Fire** (25 Valor). *Kindle.* A pillar of holy fire at the reticle: 20 damage and a burn, 3 a sec for 6 sec. Each of your bolts that hits the burning foe fans it: +1 a sec, up to +5, and refreshes it. A Smite staff turns one Holy Fire into a bonfire.

**Shadow: Mind Flay.** *Tension.*
- A cord of shadow to the first foe along the reticle, up to 3 sec. It **grows stronger the further you stand**: 4 damage a sec up close, rising to 14 a sec at 26 to 30 yd, **the taut zone**, where the cord thins, whitens and trembles. Past 30 yd it **snaps** with a whip-crack and you flinch.
- **Perfect:** a second in the taut zone. 10 Valor, and the cord rings.
- **Snap it yourself:** press Q again while it's taut and the cord whips into the foe for twice what's left of it. The Shadow Priest's cash-in.
- The foe is slowed 30% while flayed, so a chasing foe closes slowly and you keep backing off: kiting at the edge.
- Cooldown 9 sec.
- *EX* **Mind Sear** (25 Valor): the same cord, and everything within 5 yd of its foe takes half of it.

### E (close)

**Light: Holy Nova.** *A counter for others.*
- Light bursts around you, 8 yd: foes take 14, friends are healed 14. Cooldown 7 sec.
- **Perfect:** cast as a blow lands on an ally inside it: the nova's light catches the blow whole. Grace.
- *EX* **Psychic Scream** (as now).

**Shadow: Mind Blast.** *The snap.*
- A dark star on the foe under the reticle, 30 yd: 22 damage. Cooldown 8 sec.
- **Perfect:** on a foe held by a **taut** Mind Flay it snaps the cord into them for double (44), with a crack of black lightning and a beat of slow motion.
- *EX* **Shadow Word: Death** (25 Valor): 15 damage, tripled (45) on a foe under 20% health. If it doesn't kill, 10 comes back at you. A judgement, not a timing.

### 1 (utility)

**Light: Leap of Faith.** *A counter for others.*
- A ray that pulls the first ally or foe along the reticle to you (as now).
- **Perfect:** pull an ally out as a blow lands on them and it misses. Grace.
- *EX* **Mind Grip** (as now).

**Shadow: Fade.** *The perfect dodge.*
- You melt into shadow and glide 6 yd, unhittable for half a second. Cooldown 10 sec.
- **Perfect:** a blow lands where you were within 0.1 sec of the fade. Foes within 8 yd slowed 50% for 2 sec, you 40% faster for 1.5 sec, and an afterimage of you stays a moment that foes keep swinging at. Bayonetta's Witch Time, done in a world that can't stop the clock.
- *EX* **Void Shift** (25 Valor): swap places with the ally or foe under the reticle, 20 yd.

### 2 (defense)

**Light: Power Word: Shield.** *The class.*
- A shield on the ally under the reticle, 30 yd (yourself if none): absorbs 30 for 6 sec. **Two charges**, 12 sec each.
- **Perfect:** it lands on an ally within 0.1 sec before a blow lands on them (or while the blow waits on their round trip): the blow is **parried for them**. The attacker reels, open to your friend's riposte; you get a counter's 15 Valor and Grace; the shield stays.
- On yourself it only absorbs: you can't parry for yourself.
- **Lag:** judged on the Priest's screen, the press's round trip allowed.
- *EX* **Power Word: Barrier** (25 Valor): the dome, 25% less damage (as now).

**Shadow: Dispersion.**
- A column of shadow for 3 sec: 70% less damage, 5% of health back a sec, no attacking. Press again to come out. Cooldown 24 sec.
- **Perfect:** entered as a blow lands: it passes clean through you, and the striker is slowed 50% for 2 sec.
- *EX* **Vampiric Touch** (25 Valor): a clawed hand of shadow grips the foe in front of you for 8 sec; everything **you** deal it heals you for 20%.

### 3 (ultimate)

**Light: Holy Word: Salvation** (100 Valor, cooldown 40 sec). *The group check.*
- A great circle of light, 10 yd, where the Priest stands. **Everyone sees it**, friend and foe: it's the one floor mark the Priest makes, and it's the point.
- Its rim **closes on the sigil at its centre three times**, each faster: 2 sec, 1.5 sec, 1 sec. As it touches, every hero inside who **acts** (attacks, dodges, casts) within 0.1 sec, judged on their own screen, lands the beat.
- **Each beat landed** gives that hero a stack of **Holy Light**: 10% more damage a stack for 10 sec, their blows glowing gold. A hero who misses just has a smaller buff.
- **The third beat** calls a pillar of light at the centre: 30 damage, plus 15 for each hero who landed it (at most 110), and heals each of them 20. Everyone landing it is spectacular; one miss makes it a little smaller.
- The Priest gains Grace for each beat any ally lands.

**Shadow: Void Rift** (as now).

## Combos

- **Shield the Warrior as the boss swings → the boss reels → the Warrior's riposte → Grace → an instant Heal with overhealth on whoever's next.** The Light loop.
- **Penance flicked between the Rogue (heal) and her foe (harm).**
- **Holy Fire on the boss → the whole group's projectiles fan it** (a Hunter's arrows, a Mage's bolts: Kindle counts any of your own; across players is a tuning question).
- **Salvation before the group's burst:** three beats, everyone's blows golden, then a pillar.
- **Mind Flay taut at 28 yd → Mind Blast:** the snap for double.
- **Fade through a boss's swing → the boss slowed → the Warrior's heavy lands.**
- **Vampiric Touch + Mind Flay**: a Shadow Priest who heals themself by doing damage, which is the point.

## The budgets, applied

- **Q:** Penance 12 a bolt; Mind Flay 14 a sec at most (3 sec, 42), the snap at most 45.
- **E:** Holy Nova 14; Mind Blast 22, 44 snapped; Shadow Word: Death 45 at most.
- **1:** Leap of Faith and Fade deal nothing; cooldowns 10 sec and more.
- **2:** Shield's charges are 12 sec each; Dispersion 24.
- **3:** Salvation 110 at most.
- **Crowd control on heroes:** Fade's slow and Dispersion's are slows, not hard control.

## How it gets built

Three branches at once, each on top of the Mage stack (`corios/caster-counters`), each a draft PR:

1. `corios/priest-core`: staffs that click to heal (the right click as an ability, no guard), Smite, Heal, Flash Heal, the shadow staff's Pain and Drain, Power Word: Shield as a counter for others, and Grace.
2. `corios/priest-shadow`: Mind Flay's tension, Mind Blast's snap, Shadow Word: Death, Fade, Void Shift, Dispersion, Vampiric Touch.
3. `corios/priest-light`: Penance's flick, Holy Fire's Kindle, Holy Nova and Leap of Faith as counters for others, Holy Word: Salvation.

Then filmed by a PC chat with a handoff note like the Mage's.
